\begin{abstract}
This report details the implementation, evaluation, and analysis of Generative Models (Standard MVG, Tied Covariance MVG, and Naive Bayes) applied to the project dataset. It explores the validity of the Gaussian assumption, the correlation between features, and the effects of feature ablation and PCA dimensionality reduction.
