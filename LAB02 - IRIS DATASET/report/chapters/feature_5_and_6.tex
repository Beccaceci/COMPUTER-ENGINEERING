\section*{Analysis of features 5 and 6}

\subsection*{What do you observe?}
Observing the scatter plot (\texttt{features\_5\_6\_scatter.png}), the classes exhibit a highly structured, non-linear distribution. The "Fake" class forms a cross-like shape with clusters situated along the axes, while the "Genuine" class forms a ring of clusters situated in the four corners (quadrants) of the plot. Because of this geometry, the classes are heavily mixed in a way that a simple linear decision boundary would fail to separate them effectively.

\subsection*{Do the classes overlap? If so, where?}
Yes, the classes overlap extensively across almost the entire range of values. According to the computed data in \texttt{lab02\_project\_feature\_overlap\_ranges.csv}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Feature 5} overlaps between -2.0731 and 1.9972 (an overlap width of 4.0703).
    \item \textbf{Feature 6} overlaps between -2.0130 and 2.1906 (an overlap width of 4.2035).
\end{itemize}

Yes, the means are very similar and both are centered extremely close to zero. Extracted from \texttt{lab02\_project\_statistics.csv}:
\begin{itemize}
